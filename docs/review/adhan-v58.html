What the recording shows

  1. The user selects the bundled Adhan sound in Settings → Adhan & sound (الأذان والصوت).
  2. The prayer screen shows the scheduled Maghrib time, 5:24 PM.
  3. The user returns to the Android home screen. At the scheduled time, Adhan playback starts while the app is in the background.
  4. The visible playback notification offers Stop Adhan (إيقاف الأذان). The user presses it and playback stops. The prayer notification remains without the Stop action.

Why the foreground service is used

The user enables prayer alerts and chooses an Adhan audio file. At that user-configured prayer time, the app uses FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK only while the audio plays, with a visible notification and a Stop action. Deferring or interrupting playback would delay or cut off the prayer-time audio reminder.

The service stops when the audio completes or when the user presses Stop. A seven-minute safety limit also stops it. No playback service runs between prayer alerts. Silent mode is respected by default; playing in silent mode requires a separate opt-in.

Test context: Android 13 emulator; English system language and Arabic app language. Installed APK splits were generated from the version 58 AAB. The emulator clock was set just before the scheduled prayer to avoid a long wait.
